Who Is HiveHire For?

For Job Seekers

  • Build a professional profile that showcases your skills, experience, and education
  • Search and filter thousands of listings by role, location, salary, and work type
  • Apply with a tailored note and track every application in one place
  • Connect with fellow students and grow your professional network

For Recruiters

  • Post listings and reach thousands of qualified GT students and alumni
  • Search candidates by skill, location, and project experience
  • Manage your applicant pipeline with a visual Kanban board
  • Save candidate searches and directly email them through the platform
  • Reach out to candidates directly via the built-in messaging system
